# Space Chords

Interactive website for exploring musical chords. 

#### Chord Discovery

As you fly a spaceship around the map, each new spot you visit will play a new chord. Revisiting the same spot will play the same chord associated with that spot. The boundaries for these "spots" are hidden, but spots with certain chord types, such as tonic or evil, are marked with a planet.

#### Visual Effects

The vibe of the current chord being played will determine the visual environment:
* <b>Tonic:</b> Yellow background and fireworks
* <b>Peaceful:</b> Green background and wind
* <b>Melancholy:</b> Blue background and rain
* <b>Evil:</b> Red background and lightning
* <b>Dark:</b> Black background

#### Sound Design

While roaming the map, you can also control what the music sounds like. There are three instruments that can be tweaked in real time by moving the mouse and toggled on/off with the spacebar:

<b>Chords:</b> Polyphonic synth that sustains the current chord
* <b>Mouse X:</b> Cutoff of low-pass filter
* <b>Mouse Y:</b> Resonance of low-pass filter
* <b>Secondary Mouse X:</b> LFO Rate
* <b>Secondary Mouse Y:</b> LFO Depth

<b>Arpeggiator:</b> Monophonic synth that arpeggiates through the current chord
* <b>Mouse X:</b> Cutoff of low-pass filter
* <b>Mouse Y:</b> Decay time of volume envelope

<b>Kick:</b> Kick drum that plays on every beat
* <b>Mouse X:</b> Sidechain compression amount
* <b>Mouse Y:</b> Tempo
